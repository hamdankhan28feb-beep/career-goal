## Admission Information

- FAST-NUCES conducts centralized admissions for undergraduate (BS), graduate (MS), and doctoral (PhD) programs.
- Campuses: Islamabad (H-11/4 — founding campus), Lahore (Block-B, Faisal Town), Karachi (Shah Latif Town), Peshawar (Hayatabad Industrial Estate), Chiniot-Faisalabad (CFD), and Multan.
- All campuses share **one unified admission cycle**: a common entry test, a common merit formula, and a single application portal at `nu.edu.pk/admissions`.
- Admission is **strictly merit-based**; the university does not accept ECAT or NTS scores for undergraduate admissions — every applicant must sit FAST's own NU Entry Test (unless exempted via SAT/GRE, see below).
- Two intakes per year: **Fall** (larger intake) and **Spring**.
- Fall 2026 cycle: application window ran through late June 2026, entry test held in a multi-day window during June–July 2026 (exact dates/slots vary by campus, confirmed via Admit Card), classes began **19 August 2026**.

### How to Apply
1. Create an account on the admissions portal using a valid CNIC/B-form.
2. Select program level (BS or PG/Postgraduate).
3. Fill in academic information.
4. Generate and pay the test fee challan (~Rs. 1,500) via bank or online payment.
5. Download your roll number slip before the test date.
6. Sit the entry test (or submit qualifying SAT-I/GRE scores in lieu of it).
7. Check merit lists (1st, 2nd, 3rd, and final) published on the result portal.

### Merit Formula
- **50%** FAST Entry Test score
- **40%** HSSC/FSc (or equivalent) marks
- **10%** SSC marks
- Applicants may apply to multiple campuses and programs simultaneously.
- Candidates with a qualifying **SAT-I (Reasoning)** score can skip the entry test, subject to cut-off thresholds; if both scores exist, the better one is used. GRE accepted as an alternative for MS/PhD applicants (subject to percentile thresholds).

## Entry Test Information

- FAST does **not** accept ECAT or NTS scores — every applicant sits the FAST NU Entry Test, administered by the university itself.
- **Format (BSCS / BS Engineering):** Computer-based, ~2 hours, four individually-timed sections:
  - Advanced Mathematics — 50%
  - Basic Mathematics / Quantitative — 20%
  - IQ & Analytical Reasoning — 20%
  - English Comprehension & Grammar — 10%
- One breakdown cites a total of **120 MCQs** (Adv. Math 50, Basic Math 20, IQ 20, English 30) with **negative marking of −0.25 per wrong answer** — confirm current marking scheme on the official sample paper before test day.
- Once a section's timer ends (or it's submitted), you cannot return to it.
- **Business programs (BBA etc.):** Same MCQ, timed format, but weighted more toward English, Basic Mathematics, and Analytical/Logical Reasoning rather than advanced/engineering math.
- **MBA / MS (AF)** test windows are typically scheduled separately from the BS cycle (e.g., one recent cycle ran 7–18 July for MBA/MS-AF).
- Results/merit lists are checked online via `nu.edu.pk` or the dedicated result portal using Roll Number, ARN, or CNIC.
- Preparation resources: official sample papers on nu.edu.pk are recommended as the primary reference; third-party past-paper compilations (Wondar, QuizWing, Dogar Brothers, Maqsad, Parhlai) exist but vary in accuracy/currency.

## Scholarships & Financial Aid

- **Need-Based Financial Aid:** Partial to full tuition fee waivers for students who can demonstrate genuine financial need. Can be applied for during admission or later via the student portal.
- **Merit-Based Scholarships:** Awarded to top-ranked students on the merit list; described by some sources as limited in availability.
- Eligibility considers both academic performance in prior qualifications (SSC/HSSC) and admission test score.
- Aid/scholarships can be applied for at the time of admission — applying simultaneously with your application (not after) is recommended.

## Academic System & Policies

- Merit list formula: 50% entry test + 40% HSSC + 10% SSC (see Admissions above).
- No upper age limit found for BS admissions in available sources.
- HEC ranking: FAST reportedly holds the HEC W-Category (highest) ranking in Computer Science, and ranks consistently among Pakistan's top engineering universities in HEC's general rankings.
- Detailed academic policy documents (grading, probation, credit load rules, etc.) were not found in this pass — recommend pulling directly from `nu.edu.pk` for authoritative detail.
